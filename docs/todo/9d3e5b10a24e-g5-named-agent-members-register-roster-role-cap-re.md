An agent is registered on the roster like a human member, owned by the member who registers it (pair-once pattern): identity = owner plus agent name; appears on rosters as that owner's agent; share rules can name it; role on any item capped by the owner's; removing or demoting the owner cascades to the owner's agents. Today bashy meet can invite an agent locally but the seat is not tied to circle/team admission and cannot act on apps or documents. Integrate on one host first: the registered agent joins a room and edits a shared Notepad item through the G2/G3 verbs under its own identity, with provenance showing owner and agent. Cross-outpost is Sprint 685 X1/X2. Public wire contract only for anything the satellite does. Contract: G1, docs/plan-group-participation.md.
