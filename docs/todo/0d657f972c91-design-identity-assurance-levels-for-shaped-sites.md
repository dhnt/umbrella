Operator 2026-11-18, plan and design only, for the future. Borrow the marketplace pattern (freelancer-style verified badges). A second axis next to the relationship: anonymous, authenticated (pseudonym), email (consent or membership), verified (third-party identity verification such as a government ID and photo match). Rules: an audience may require a minimum level; the circle still bounds and levels only narrow; cloudbox stores only the outcome (level, provider, date) as relationship data, never documents, ID numbers or photos; sites receive the level, never the evidence; verification is opt-in through an external provider.
