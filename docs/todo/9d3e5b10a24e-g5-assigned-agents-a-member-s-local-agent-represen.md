A member assigns an agent to work on their behalf. It stays local on the member's outpost, is never a roster row, and carries exactly the member's identity, admission and permissions; provenance records the agent name beside the member. Every agent is bound to one cloudbox owner or member email; a team-wide agent is simply the owner's assigned agent. Other participants see it through room or shared-session presence as that member's agent, and @NAME (G6) resolves to it. Removing or demoting the member removes or caps the agent by construction. Budget follows the member. Today bashy meet can invite an agent locally but the seat is not tied to circle/team admission and cannot act on apps or documents: integrate on one host first, the assigned agent joins a room and edits a shared Notepad item through the G2/G3 verbs under the member's identity. No cloudbox schema or release needed; there are no owner-registered roster agents. Cross-outpost is Sprint 685 X1/X2. Contract: G1, docs/plan-group-participation.md.
