Operator 2026-11-14: ai.dhnt.io showcases software shaping by inviting people to start the journey — practice beside traditional learning and training. The story it tells is 'hey, you can try it now, here'.

Scope (the MVP's dogfood target, in place of shaping the company page):
  - the site is a shaped app registered from its own repo (638 S3), with the Shape link on the page (638 S2) and the page prefilled on arrival (638 S1);
  - the invitation is the page's own content: a lesson or a short exercise on the left, the thing to change on the right, and the wish box already pointed at it;
  - which parts a visitor may change is the owner's feature list (M4), published so the page can say it in words: these you can try, these are the site's contract;
  - a visitor's approved change is their own version only — others and anonymous readers see the base, which the engine already does;
  - practice is resettable and budgeted (M6);
  - the traditional half is ordinary content in the same repo, so improving a lesson is itself a wish.

Acceptance: a person who is not the owner lands on ai.dhnt.io, is invited, wishes (typed or spoken), sees Red then Green receipts, previews, approves, sees their own version, resets, and nothing they did is visible to the next visitor; a wish at the site's contract is refused in the page's own words.
